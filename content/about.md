I work inside the Linux kernel at [Qualcomm](https://www.qualcomm.com/) — device drivers, inter-processor communication, network subsystems. Before that, IoT gateways, bootloaders, and bare-metal firmware.

Every week I write one deep dive that takes something every engineer "knows" and shows what actually happens underneath.

But I'm not just a kernel person.

I'm endlessly curious about how things work — not just computers, but systems in general. How cities move people. How languages shape thought. How a good book rewires the way you see a problem. I read widely, tinker constantly, and collect interests the way some people collect browser tabs.

This site is my workshop — a place to think out loud and connect dots across disciplines. If you stay up too late chasing a stack trace *or* a good idea, you'll feel at home here.
